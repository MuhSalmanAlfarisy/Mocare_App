# Mocare

**Mocare (Motor Care)** is a motorcycle care application born from a personal frustration.

I often forgot to refuel my motorcycle, and there were times when it even ran out of fuel while I was on the road. That experience gave me the idea to build an application that could help motorcycle owners keep track of their fuel usage, mileage, and maintenance.

> **Note:** Mocare is still in its very early stages.

The project is far from finished, and there is still a lot of research, experimentation, and planning needed to determine the best features, user flow, and overall approach.

For now, Mocare is still an ongoing exploration of how I can turn this idea into a useful and practical motorcycle care application.
